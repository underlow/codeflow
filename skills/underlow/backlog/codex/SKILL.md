---
name: backlog
description: Record explicitly deferred work and confirmed out-of-scope bugs with enough context to resume later. Review repository backlog at session start or when asked what to do next. Do not turn rejected ideas or unsolicited improvements into tasks.
---

Use `$backlog [request or item ID]`, or apply this skill when work is deferred.
With no argument, review open items and offer a next task without starting it.
Read [the shared workflow](references/backlog.md) before acting.
