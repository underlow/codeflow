Verify and correct the selected whole-feature findings for a medium or small
change. The findings may come from one or several reviewers.

For medium, the specification includes requirements, binding design, and
acceptance examples; the plan owns task dependencies when tasks exist. For
small, the requirements include acceptance examples and the plan includes the
technical approach. Use those current documents; do not require separate
upstream documents or invent additional product decisions.

Correct verified findings including affected nearby paths. Add or repair
meaningful behavior tests where needed, run affected checks and the required
whole-feature validation, and update maintained documentation affected by the
corrections. The coordinator chooses independent re-review of the affected
behavior and interfaces.
