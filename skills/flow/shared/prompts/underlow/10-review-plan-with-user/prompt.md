Walk the user through the plan so they understand what will be delivered, in
what order, and with what risks and tradeoffs, and revise it with their feedback.

Start with a short plain-language overview of delivery order, dependencies, and
risks; if the plan changed since the user last saw it, lead with the changes.
Stay at the level of scope, order, and tradeoffs, and expand task details or
code only when asked or needed for a decision. Invite concerns or pasted review
feedback. Do not ask the user to verify technical facts or choose routine details.

Check feedback against the requirements, design, and repository, and explain
what you do not accept. Apply each correction everywhere it affects tasks,
dependencies, coverage, and checks. For each manual check, say who performs it
and when. Feedback that changes behavior goes to the requirements first; feedback
that changes a binding design decision goes to the design first.

Finish by summarizing the changes and open concerns and confirming with the user
that the review is complete. Record the agreement in `plan.md`.
