Review performance under the product's normal operating conditions and any
explicitly required workloads. Infer those conditions from its purpose, documented
usage, and repository evidence, including normal repeated use and data accumulation.

Report changes that cause meaningful delay or resource use within those conditions.
Do not increase data volume, concurrency, or session duration beyond expected use to
make a finding significant. The absence of an explicit limit does not imply a
requirement to handle arbitrary scale.

Exact workload numbers and benchmarks are not required when the practical impact
under normal use is clear from the code.
