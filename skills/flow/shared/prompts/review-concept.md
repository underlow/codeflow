Review the concept for problems that would change its purpose, scope,
feasibility, or a major product choice.

Check that the problem, outcome, boundaries, exclusions, major choices, and
journeys fit together. Test important assumptions against the repository and the
ways users reach the feature. For each journey, consider input, prerequisites,
side effects, failures, and final state; when a shared assumption fails, check
every journey that depends on it.

In scope: an interaction with the current system that breaks a stated journey or
adds a consequential side effect. Out of scope: wording, and details that
requirements can settle without changing the concept. Respect explicit user
decisions unless new evidence changes their meaning.
